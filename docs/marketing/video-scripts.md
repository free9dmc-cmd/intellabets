# Short video scripts — YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels

LAUNCH-PLAN → Go-to-market → Channels, #3: "showing the vig calculation on a
real slate is inherently demonstrable". These are scripts for the **owner to
record**. Each runs 45–75 seconds, on screen recording with a voice-over. No
face on camera needed.

## Rules for every video

- **Real prices are fine; results are not.** Showing what books are charging
  today is public information. Never show or say a win rate, record, units won,
  ROI, a "hit", a screenshot of a winning slip, or anything like it, including
  in captions, pinned comments and replies.
- **No picks.** The point is the arithmetic, not a bet. Don't say "take this".
- **Say "illustrative" when the numbers aren't live**, and blur or crop
  sportsbook logos unless the platform's rules and the books' brand terms allow
  showing them.
- **On screen for the whole video, small but legible:** "21+ · Gambling
  involves risk · 1-800-GAMBLER".
- **Video description:**
  > Educational. IntellaBets is not a sportsbook and does not take bets.
  > 21+. Bet only what you can afford to lose. Help: 1-800-GAMBLER.
  > Free no-vig calculator: https://intellabets.com/devig-calculator
- **Check each platform's gambling-content rules first.** Some restrict
  gambling-related content by region or require age-gating the account.

## Script 1: "-110 / -110 isn't a coin flip" (≈60 s)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | A game line, both sides -110 | "Both sides at -110. Fifty-fifty, right? Not quite." |
| 5–20 s | Implied probability formula, 110 ÷ 210 = 52.4% on each side | "Turn each price into a probability: 110 divided by 210 is 52.4 percent. Both sides." |
| 20–30 s | 52.4% + 52.4% = 104.8%, with the extra 4.8% highlighted | "Add them up and you get 104.8 percent. That extra 4.8 is the book's cut. It's built into every line." |
| 30–45 s | Each side divided by 104.8% gives 50.0% / 50.0%, fair price +100 | "Take the cut out and you're back to 50/50, a fair price of plus 100. That's the real line underneath." |
| 45–60 s | Calculator page, entering -110 / -110 | "This works for any line. There's a free calculator if you don't want to do it by hand. It still doesn't tell you who wins. It just shows you what you're paying." |

## Script 2: "Same bet, three prices" (≈50 s)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Three books' prices on the same side, labeled illustrative: -120, -110, +100 | "Same team, same game, three books." |
| 5–25 s | $100 stake: wins pay $83.33 / $90.91 / $100.00 | "A hundred dollars at minus 120 wins 83. At minus 110, 91. At plus 100, a full hundred." |
| 25–40 s | Break-even win rate: 54.5% / 52.4% / 50.0% | "Better price, less often you need to win just to break even: 54.5 percent down to 50." |
| 40–50 s | "A better price ≠ a likelier win" | "It doesn't make the bet more likely to win. A loss costs the same either way. You just get paid more when you're right." |

Numbers match https://intellabets.com/line-shopping, where they are computed
from the site's own tested code.

## Script 3: "Why we don't post our record" (≈45 s)

Optional, for accounts where people ask. It reuses the honest answer from
`forum-posts.md` draft 2.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 s | "What's your record?" | "People ask what our record is. We don't post one yet." |
| 10–30 s | Closing line value, one-line definition | "What matters for a price-based method is whether you beat the closing price, not a short run of wins. Our sample since we fixed how closing prices are recorded is too small to mean anything." |
| 30–45 s | "We'll publish it with the sample size" | "When it's big enough, we'll publish it, with the sample size next to it. If someone shows you a record with no sample size, ask why." |
