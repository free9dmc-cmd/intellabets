## 2. Order to do this in

**Affiliate programs first, then state registrations.** Affiliate forms are faster. And in most states the operator sponsors your filing: it files the form for you, or it must sign a letter or certificate before you can file.

1. **Fill in the Start here table** (section 1).
2. **Send the easy messages first.** These create no account and need no signature.
   - DraftKings interest survey (section 3.1). Only your email is required.
   - BetMGM contact form (section 3.3). It asks for their state checklist and their deep-link spec.
   - bet365 contact form (section 3.5). It asks whether a pre-launch US site should apply yet.
   - Rush Affiliates (BetRivers): email affiliates@rush-affiliates.com before registering (section 3.8).
   - Caesars: open its affiliate site in a normal web browser (section 3.10). We could not load it.
3. **Create the BetMGM Partners account** (section 3.4). BetMGM's FAQ does not list a state licence as a condition of registering: it prompts for NJ vendor registration during sign-up or through your account manager. (The contact message in section 3.3 asks BetMGM to confirm this.) You cannot promote or get links until you hold the state licence and a signed agreement exists. Creating the account is the application, and it includes agreeing to their terms.
4. **When an operator accepts you, start that state's filing with them** (section 4). Use the table below to compare states.
5. **Only after a state filing exists:** register with Rush Affiliates (tick only the states you hold), apply to betPARX (it requires a licence in MD, MI, NJ or PA), and tick only those states anywhere a form asks.
6. **Wait on bet365's full application** until the site has measurable traffic (sections 3.6 and 3.7). After a rejection you must wait 30 days to apply again.
7. **Decide on Betr** before doing anything (section 3.2). It is not a real-money sportsbook.

### 4.1 New Jersey: Vendor Registration

**What it is.** A vendor registration under N.J.S.A. 5:12-92c(1). It is not the higher "ancillary casino service industry enterprise" (CSIE) licence. It has two parts: the Vendor Registration Form (Form 40) and the Vendor Registration Supplemental Disclosure Form (VRSDF, Form 41). DGE rulings confirm that an affiliate paid only by CPA or a fixed fee stays a vendor registrant. Only revenue-share affiliates need the ancillary CSIE licence (Order No. 2665, SharpLink, 14 May 2024; Order No. 2988, Cyan Blue/Oddschecker). DGE must approve any later move to revenue share before it starts.

**Who files.** Both of you.

- You complete Form 40, but the casino licensee files it with DGE. The form is only available from the casino you first plan to work with. In practice, that is the NJ casino or online sports pool licensee whose affiliate program accepts you.
- You file Form 41 yourself, directly with DGE, within 30 days of the Form 40 filing.
- Separately, each NJ platform provider certifies its affiliates to DGE every year by December 31. That is the operator's filing, not yours.

## 5. Things you must not say

These rules apply to every form, email, call and page.

1. **No audience numbers.** Users: 0. Monthly visitors: none to report yet. Revenue: $0. Do not estimate, round up or forecast traffic. Where a form needs a number, type 0.
2. **No win rates, ROI or profit claims.** Do not say how much users win or will win.
3. **Never use "risk-free", "free bet", "guaranteed" or "lock".**
4. **Do not call unfinished features live.** These are *in progress*, not done:
   - state-based gating of betslip links,
   - a helpline beside every betslip link,
   - an affiliate disclosure.
5. **Do not claim registrations, licences or operator deals you do not have.** Do not tick a state on any form (BetMGM, bet365 regions, Rush brands, betPARX states) unless your registration there is filed or approved. (Illinois, Ohio and North Carolina need no filing from a CPA or flat-fee affiliate; tick those only once the operator agrees, and never on betPARX, whose question asks where you are licensed.)
6. **Do not tell bet365 that "no licences are required"** unless every state you choose is Illinois, Ohio or North Carolina. Most US states require registration.
7. **No revenue share in Illinois, Massachusetts or New York.** In Massachusetts operators may not pay affiliates a percentage at all. In New York, fixed fee only.
8. **Do not claim paid or bulk traffic.** No paid ads at launch, no purchased email lists, no incentivized or bonus-abuse traffic. On bet365, do not tick Paid search, paid social, Email marketing or Sub-affiliation.
9. **Do not promise placement.** Betslip links are ranked purely by payout. An operator ranks first only when it pays the most.
10. **Do not link or promote any book that is not licensed.** That includes offshore books, and possibly Betr (not a real-money sportsbook).
11. **Do not make up any owner-only fact.** If you do not know an answer (a social handle, a date, a number), leave it blank or write "None yet" if that is true.
